# The 2026 MLOps & Production LLM Infrastructure Roadmap

Welcome to the **MLOps & Production LLM Infrastructure Roadmap Playlist**.

This specialized systems path guides platform engineers, SREs, and machine learning practitioners through the architecture patterns required to serve, fine-tune, auto-scale, and evaluate private, open-weights AI models in enterprise cloud environments.

### What You Will Master:
1. **vLLM & Inference Serving**: Continuous batching, PagedAttention, and tensor parallelism.
2. **DeepSeek-R1 & GRPO Alignment**: MLA low-rank KV-cache compression and critic-free reinforcement learning.
3. **GPU Cluster Management & FinOps**: Kubernetes pod self-healing, eBPF kernel observability, and DCGM cost right-sizing.
4. **Automated Evals & Guardrails**: LangSmith golden benchmark datasets, OpenTelemetry distributed tracing, and rate-limiting circuit breakers.
